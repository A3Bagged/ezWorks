# Introduction <Badge type="warning" text="Beta" />

## What is K1V?

**K1 Vector (K1V)** is a complete linear-rail gantry upgrade for Creality K-Series printers.<br>
It replaces the stock XY system with a rigid extrusion frame, MGN12 linear rails, redesigned CoreXY belt routing.

![K1V Gantry](/K1V_V1-2.png)

The result is a stiffer, more accurate motion platform built for higher speeds, cleaner surfaces, sharper details, and more consistent prints. If you want to push your K-Series beyond its factory limits, this is where you start.

## Why should you use K1V?

- **Full linear-rail gantry.** Replaces the stock rods and LM8UU linear bearings with MGN12 linear rails.
- **Live-shaft idlers.** A robust, smoothly rotating idler system for the CoreXY belt path.
- **Greater rigidity and stability.** The extrusion-based frame cuts gantry flex and unwanted movement.
- **Built for demanding motion.** A stable platform for higher speeds, acceleration, and precision tuning.
- **Designed for the K-Series.** Purpose-built around Creality K1-Series printers, and compatible with most toolheads made for front-mounted linear rails.
- **Open source and community supported.** Actively maintained, with build help available on Discord.

::: warning Before you begin

K1V is a moderately difficult conversion. It requires drilling and minor adjustments to the K1 frame, and it may void your printer warranty.<br><br>
If you're comfortable with basic mechanical work, you'll be right at home. If not, take your time (and maybe don't rush it at 2am). Support is available in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/cUgSp74aAp).
:::
